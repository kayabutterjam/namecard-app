One-time setup

Connect your Anthropic key

Reading a card costs a fraction of a cent, billed to your own Anthropic account. Your key is stored only in this browser and is sent straight to Anthropic — it never reaches this app's database or servers.

  1. Open console.anthropic.com/settings/keys and sign in, creating an account if you need one.
  2. Make sure the account has credit — Billing in the left sidebar. A few dollars lasts thousands of cards.
  3. Click Create Key, name it something like namecards, and copy it. It starts with sk-ant- and is shown only once.
  4. Paste it below. It is checked against Anthropic before it is saved.
